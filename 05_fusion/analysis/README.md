# Fusion analysis

`results/fusion_overall_metrics.csv`, `fusion_sca_metrics.csv`, and
`fusion_cwe_metrics.csv` compare independent LLM, frozen Stage 1, and final
EvoVerify separately for DeepSeek-v4-flash and GPT-6 Luna. CWE groups overlap.
`fusion_vs_baseline_comparison.csv` gives same-model metric differences against
the independent LLM and Stage 1. `stage2_revision_analysis.json` counts correct
and incorrect Stage 1 to Fusion flips and retentions, plus the reported evolution
evidence reliability categories. `label_transitions.json` lists every TP/FP
transition, warning ID, and GT split. All files are rebuilt from the one released
GT and frozen predictions by `04_analysis/scripts/build_final_paper_sync.py`.

`results/verification_consistency_audit.json` is generated by the offline
Fusion validator. It lists status/label conflicts and reports 14 for DeepSeek,
13 for GPT-6 Luna. The audit does not alter predictions or their evaluation.
